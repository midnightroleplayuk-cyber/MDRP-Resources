# :hamburger: Pickle Restaurants goes live tomorrow

*A restaurant job where the kitchen is a place you work, not a progress bar you stare at.*

**:receipt: Say goodbye to the config.lua** - *99% of configuration is now done in-game, no restart required.*
* Restaurants, menus, recipes, props, staff, roles and payouts all live in the database
* A new dish is a form you fill in, not a file edit and a restart

**:man_cook: Hands on cooking** - *You drag ingredients into the world and cook them yourself.*
* Patties cook on both sides, cheese melts in stages, baskets lower and oven doors shut
* Build food layer by layer and take it apart later exactly how it went together
* Pour at a seven tap soda fountain where ice falls into the cup and flavors stream in
* Box it up in trays and takeout boxes with hinged lids that swing open on click
* Squeeze bottles flip over onto the food, frosting tools rub across a cake until it's covered
* Burn something and leave it there and the station catches a real fire that ejects the cook

**:tv: In world screens** - *Menus, boards, kiosks and the till are painted onto the props themselves.*
* Order at a kiosk with a cursor on the actual screen
* The cashier's POS is a live till on the counter, no fullscreen UI in your face

**:art: Your own brand** - *Every restaurant looks like itself.*
* Upload art once and it lands on packaging, cups, boxes, boards and the kiosk
* Pick one color and the whole interface rebuilds around it

**:fork_knife_plate: Sit down and eat** - *Food is something you actually consume.*
* Your tray lays out on the table arranged exactly how the cook made it
* Eat in bites, and a half eaten burger is still half eaten tomorrow

**:red_car: NPCs and deliveries** - *The restaurant runs even when it's quiet.*
* Customers walk in, order, and leave happy or annoyed with a face to match
* Deliveries end at a real doorstep with a knock and a hand off

**:mobile_phone: Phone app** - *Order food to your door from your phone, built for lb-phone.*
* Customers order, the kitchen sees tickets arrive live
* Owner finances, staff clock in, levels and achievements

**:white_check_mark: Why us** - *Most restaurant scripts hand you three burgers in a config file and call it a job.*
* Author a food once and it works in the kitchen, register, kiosk, boards, phone and delivery
* Variants never spam your item list, every drink and doughnut stays one item
* ESX, QBCore and standalone, with ox_inventory, qb-inventory and seven others

**Live tomorrow.** Bring an appetite. :cucumber:
