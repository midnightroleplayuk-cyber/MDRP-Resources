HUNK-AC prop whitelist for tw-builderjob (Twostone Builders)

The job spawns custom construction props. With HUNK_AC.OnlyWhitelistedProps
set to true these are removed and the player is kicked, which shows up in
game as "the tool cannot be placed" until the anti-cheat is disabled.

Add the numbers below to HUNK_AC.WhitelistedProps in the client config
(client-configs/props). 251 models, unsigned JOAAT hashes.

Alternative if you prefer not to maintain a list:
  HUNK_AC.OnlyWhitelistedProps = false

MaxPropsPerUser does not need raising: a player holds at most three
networked props at a time (tool, carried piece, remote). Every other prop
the job creates is client-local and invisible to the check.
