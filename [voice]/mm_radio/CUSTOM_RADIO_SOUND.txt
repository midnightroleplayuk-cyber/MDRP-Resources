CUSTOM RADIO PTT SOUNDS
=======================

Place these two files directly inside the mm_radio folder:

    mm_radio/radio_speak.ogg
    mm_radio/radio_end.ogg

Behaviour:
- radio_speak.ogg plays when you PRESS the push-to-talk button / start transmitting.
- radio_end.ogg plays when you RELEASE the push-to-talk button / stop transmitting.

The pma-voice stock mic click is disabled so it does not overlap these sounds.
The existing Enable Clicks setting in mm_radio controls these custom sounds.

After adding both OGG files, restart mm_radio.
