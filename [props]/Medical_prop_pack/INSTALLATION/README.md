# **BBS MEDICAL PROP PACK**
*A small pack of props to help your server's immersiveness* 

---

## 🐛 Installation

1. **Extract**  
   Unzip into your server's resources. Ensure in your `server.cfg`

2. **Item Images**  
   Place the provided item images into your inventory's `images` folder.
   
3. **Inventory Setup**  
   Add the items to your inventory script. (You can create the items yourself)
